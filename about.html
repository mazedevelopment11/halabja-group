Chairman's Message

A word from
Aso Bamoki

Since our founding in Halabja in 1992, we have been driven by a singular mission: to contribute to the prosperity and development of our people and our homeland.

Our overarching vision is to contribute to a better country, thriving on sustainable progress and lasting prosperity — for both current and future generations.

"Our goal is to be a trusted provider of services and businesses that support the country's economic growth and development. By providing cutting-edge technology, creating job opportunities, and supporting local talent, we intend to leverage our expertise, resources, and strengths to improve the lives of citizens."
— Aso Bamoki, Chairman · Halabja Group

Chairman's Message

A word from
Aso Bamoki

Over the past few decades, Iraq has faced considerable instability and enduring political and economic challenges, which have profoundly impacted the nation's development across all spheres of life. As an esteemed entity, we take great pride in being part of the ongoing efforts for the reconstruction and restoration of Kurdistan and Iraq, following prolonged periods of instability and devastation.

Our journey originated with the establishment of Halabja Group for Trading in the late 1980s. Since then, driven by unwavering dedication, we have expanded our scope beyond the trading sector, venturing into a diverse range of industries — spanning the industrial, construction, real estate, telecommunication, education, and retail sectors, all unified under the banner of Halabja Group.

At the heart of our mission lies an unwavering commitment to serving our customers and stakeholders with utmost dedication and fidelity, while consistently elevating the quality of our products to meet the highest standards. We firmly believe that knowledge serves as the key to greater success, building trust among the people, and propelling the country's development in the most efficient and effective manner.

"Our goal is to be a trusted provider of services and businesses that support the country's economic growth and development. By providing cutting-edge technology, creating job opportunities, and supporting local talent, we intend to leverage our expertise, resources, and strengths to improve the lives of citizens."
— Aso Bamoki, Chairman · Halabja Group

Our overarching vision is to contribute to a better country, thriving on sustainable progress and lasting prosperity — for both current and future generations.

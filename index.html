Who We Are

A Legacy of Excellence

Halabja Group was founded with a single vision: to be the most trusted partner in the development of Kurdistan's infrastructure and economy. Over two decades, we have grown from a regional contractor into a diversified enterprise with operations spanning construction, petroleum, and international trade.

Headquartered in Halabja — a city defined by resilience and rebirth — we carry that spirit in everything we build, every contract we honor, and every community we serve.
